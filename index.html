Bioinformatics and scientific software professional with 15+ years of experience at the intersection of computational biology, clinical genomics, next-generation sequencing and software engineering.

My expertise spans clinical bioinformatics, whole-genome and whole-exome sequencing data analysis, transcriptomics, genome assembly, variant annotation and prioritisation, and automated bioinformatics pipeline development. I bring strong programming and automation expertise, with hands-on experience in Python, JavaScript, Nextflow, Linux and workflow orchestration, translating complex biological requirements into scalable and reproducible computational solutions. At Thermo Fisher Scientific, I contribute to scientific software development for Research Use Only (RUO) instruments, incorporating 21 CFR Part 11 requirements, and the GeneART digital platform for online DNA synthesis ordering. Combining genomics expertise with software engineering, workflow automation and product strategy, I am passionate about building intelligent, data-driven solutions and applying machine learning approaches to address complex challenges in genomics and life sciences.
